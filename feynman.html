ElectromagneticExchange particle: virtual photon, γ. Acts between charged particles. Infinite range.
WeakExchange particles: W⁺ and W⁻ bosons. They have rest mass, so the range is tiny (about 10⁻¹⁸ m). Changes quark or lepton type.
Strong (between nucleons)Exchange particle: the pion. Felt by hadrons only. Range about 3 fm.
How to read a Feynman diagram
  1. Time runs up the page. Particles enter at the bottom and leave at the top. Left and right only separate the particles; they are not a distance scale.
  2. Straight lines are particles such as electrons, neutrinos and nucleons. The arrow shows the direction each particle travels.
  3. The wavy line is the exchange particle (gauge boson). It carries momentum, energy and, for W bosons, charge from one particle to the other.
  4. Each vertex is an interaction. Charge, baryon number and lepton number must be the same just before and just after every vertex.
  5. W bosons change particle type. A d quark becomes a u quark, or an electron becomes an electron neutrino. Photons and pions leave the particle unchanged.
