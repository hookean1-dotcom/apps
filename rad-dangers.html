Dangers of Radioactivity

The difference

Irradiation

Radiation reaches an object from a source outside it. It stops when the source is removed or shielded. The object does not become radioactive.

Contamination

Radioactive atoms get onto or into an object or person. Radiation keeps coming until the material is removed or has decayed.

Contamination is usually more dangerous: you cannot walk away from it, and inside the body the radiation is absorbed by living cells.

Reducing the risk

In the school laboratory

Handle sources with long tongs and hold them at arm’s length. Never point a source at anyone. Return it to its lead-lined box as soon as possible. Do not eat or drink, and wash your hands afterwards.

At work

Wear a dosimeter badge to record the dose received. Stand behind lead or concrete screens, use remote handling for strong sources, wear lead aprons where needed and keep exposure times short.
