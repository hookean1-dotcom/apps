Why so rare?

    The real experiments

    In 1909 Geiger and Marsden found that about 1 in 8000 alphas were reflected back from a platinum plate. The plate was thick, so alphas could be turned back from deep inside it, and they slowed down on the way in, which makes large deflections more likely.

    In 1911 Rutherford quoted about 1 in 20,000 alphas turned through an average angle of 90° by gold foil 0.00004 cm (0.4 µm) thick. Choose “Rutherford’s gold foil” and fire a million to compare. The model predicts the same order of magnitude.

    The model ignores the atom’s electrons, which are far too light to turn an alpha back, and the energy alphas lose inside the foil.
