Rutherford’s surprise

In 1909 Geiger and Marsden fired alpha particles at gold foil. Almost all went straight through, just like these darts. About 1 in 8000 bounced back by more than 90° – the foil was over a thousand atoms thick, and the positive nucleus repels an alpha particle from a distance. In 1911 Rutherford explained this with a tiny, dense nucleus.
