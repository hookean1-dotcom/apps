The equations

VpVs=NpNs VpIp = VsIs(100% efficient) ε = N ΔΦΔt efficiency = VsIsVpIp × 100%

Both coils share one core flux, so every turn of either coil has the same rate of change of flux, ΔΦ/Δt — the induced voltages are therefore in the ratio of the turns. Lenz's law sets the direction of the induced e.m.f. Real cores are laminated to cut eddy-current losses.
