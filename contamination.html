    Side by side

    IrradiationContamination
    What it isAn object is exposed to nuclear radiation from a source outside it.Unwanted radioactive atoms get onto or into an object.
    Does the object become radioactive?No. It gives out no radiation of its own.It carries radioactive atoms, so it gives out radiation wherever it goes.
    When exposure stopsAs soon as the source is shielded or taken away.Only when the atoms are removed or have decayed.
    ProtectionShielding, more distance, less time near the source.Gloves, tongs and protective clothing so atoms cannot transfer. Wash off or remove anything contaminated.
    Most hazardous radiationGamma and beta, because they can travel through air and into the body.Alpha, if the atoms get inside the body. It is the most ionising.
    ExampleGamma rays used to sterilise surgical instruments.Radioactive dust on clothing, or breathing in radon gas.
