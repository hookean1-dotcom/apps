The seven & the signposts

6.2 — the seven to know

Cassiopeia, Cygnus, Orion, the Plough, the Southern Cross, the Summer Triangle and the Square of Pegasus. Learn each shape and its brightest stars, then practise drawing them from memory.

6.3 — the signposts

Plough → Polaris (the two Pointer stars) and Arcturus (arc off the handle). Orion's Belt → Sirius (down-left), Aldebaran and the Pleiades (up-right). Square of Pegasus → Fomalhaut (south) and the Andromeda Galaxy (from Alpheratz).

Why signposts help

The bright patterns are easy to find, so once you know them you can 'hop' from a familiar shape to a fainter star, cluster or galaxy nearby — exactly how astronomers star-hop.

Different names, same stars (6.4)

The Plough is also the Big Dipper or part of the Great Bear — different cultures named the same patterns in different ways.
