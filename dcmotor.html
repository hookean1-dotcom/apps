Torque against angle

With the commutator the torque never reverses, so the coil keeps turning the same way.

The physics

Force on each side (N turns): F = N B I L Turning effect on the coil: τ = N B I A cos θ Fleming's left-hand rule: First finger = Field (N→S), seCond finger = Current, thuMb = Motion (force). The commutator reverses the current in the coil every half-turn, just as the coil passes the vertical, so the forces keep turning it the same way. Switch it off to swap in slip rings and watch the coil stall.
