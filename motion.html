Every stage has a constant acceleration, which is all any exam graph ever asks for. A stage with a = 0 is constant velocity; a negative a while moving forwards is a deceleration.

What to plot

Displacement and velocity carry a sign; distance and speed never go down and never go negative. Swapping between them is the quickest way to see the difference.
