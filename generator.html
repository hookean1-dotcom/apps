The physics

EMF induced in the coil: ε = N B A ω cos θ Peak EMF and frequency: ε0 = B A N ω  ·  f = rpm ÷ 60 Fleming's right-hand rule: thuMb = Motion, First finger = Field (N→S), seCond finger = induced Current. The EMF is greatest when the coil plane is parallel to the field, where the sides cut the field lines fastest and the flux linkage is changing fastest (passing through zero). The coil's EMF always alternates: slip rings pass it on as AC, while the split ring swaps the connections every half-turn to give a one-way, varying DC.
