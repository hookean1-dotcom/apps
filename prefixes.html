Golden rules

  1. Bigger unit → smaller number. An amp is much bigger than a milliamp, so you need fewer of them: 50 mA becomes 0.05 A. Going to a smaller unit makes the number bigger.
  2. Convert to SI units before you substitute. Change every quantity into its SI unit (A, V, Ω, m, s, kg, J, W, Hz…) first, then put the numbers into the equation.
  3. The SI unit of mass is the kilogram, not the gram. Grams almost always need converting: 250 g = 0.25 kg.
  4. Squared and cubed units square and cube the conversion. 1 m = 100 cm, so 1 m² = 100 × 100 = 10 000 cm² and 1 m³ = 1 000 000 cm³.
  5. Time is not powers of ten. 1 minute = 60 s and 1 hour = 3600 s.
