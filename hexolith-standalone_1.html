Hexolith

Step · then strike

A game for two on a honeycomb that gives way beneath you. Step to a neighbouring hex, then strike one anywhere on the board: whole hexes fracture, fractured hexes fall. Whoever runs out of footing loses.

Ochre to move

How it goes

  1. Step your piece to one of the six neighbouring hexes — any hex that hasn't fallen will take you.
  2. Then strike a hex anywhere on the board. A whole one fractures; a fractured one gives way.
  3. Neither piece's own hex can be struck.
  4. Run out of hexes to step to and you lose.

The three states

  • Whole — safe footing
  • Fractured — holds you once, then goes
  • Fallen — nothing left to stand on

Fine print

Standing on a fractured hex is borrowed time: it carries you for exactly one turn and gives way the moment you leave.

Once per game each player may vault a gap — hop in a straight line over exactly one fallen hex and land beyond it. You still strike afterwards.

Ochre starts bottom-right and plays for you. Indigo starts top-left.

Indigo wins

Ochre had nowhere left to step.
