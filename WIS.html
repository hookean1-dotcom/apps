Books about women in science

Topic numbers follow the AQA GCSE specifications (8461, 8462, 8463); WJEC, Eduqas and Edexcel cover the same ideas. "Beyond GCSE" collects women from the reading list whose work sits outside the science specifications. Portraits are illustrated tiles, not photographs: use the Wikipedia link on each profile to see real photos. Biographies are written for this resource and checked against Wikipedia, October 2026. Book tags show which scientists are profiled in Women in Science (Ignotofsky), Headstrong (Swaby), The Glass Universe (Sobel) and Hidden Figures (Shetterly).
