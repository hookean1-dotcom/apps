When is an e.m.f. induced?
Only while the flux linking the secondary is changing. The faster it changes, the larger the e.m.f.
Reading field lines
Lines keep the same shape: a stronger field means more, closer lines, not bigger ones. An ideal iron core keeps all the field inside it; real cores leak a few per cent.
Does frequency matter?
With a fixed supply p.d. (like mains), a higher frequency gives a smaller peak flux that reverses more often, so the rate of change, and the secondary e.m.f., stay the same: Vs/Vp = Ns/Np. Compare with a fixed current.
