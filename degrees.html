    Zooming in on the angle

    Half a degree is 30′, not 50′

    23.5° means 23 and a half degrees. Half of 60 is 30, so 23.5° = 23° 30′. Never copy the decimal digits across as arcminutes.

    Arcseconds: divide by 3600

    When going back to decimal degrees, divide arcminutes by 60 and arcseconds by 3600, because 1° = 60 × 60 = 3600″.

    Right ascension uses time

    Right ascension is given in hours, minutes and seconds (24 h = 360°, so 1 h = 15°). Those minutes are not arcminutes. Declination uses ° ′ ″.
